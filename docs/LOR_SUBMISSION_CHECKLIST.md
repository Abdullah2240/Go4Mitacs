# Mitacs Letter of Recommendation - Private Checklist

Source guide: [`GRI-Recommendation-letter-guidelines_2025-1.pdf`](GRI-Recommendation-letter-guidelines_2025-1.pdf). This is a preparation checklist for this repository; it is not a Mitacs submission form.

## Ask the referee for a letter that is

- On official university letterhead.
- Signed by the recommending supervisor.
- Written in English or French.
- Complete with the referee's title, department affiliation, university, email address, and phone number.

## Brief the referee on the substance to cover

- The period of supervision and its dates.
- How many comparable students/researchers the referee supervised during that period.
- A comparative assessment of Abdullah's productivity and performance, with specific contributions to research, academic discussion, or collaborative work. Publications can be mentioned only if applicable.
- Notable achievements, awards/honours, high-impact results, or distinctive contributions.
- Where genuine, work ethic, teamwork, leadership, and mentoring.
- An explicit overall endorsement of the application and confidence in future potential.

## Submission / mailing procedure

The supplied guideline does **not** state an email recipient, subject line, filename, portal, deadline, or whether the applicant or referee must submit the letter. Therefore:

1. Prepare a signed PDF on institutional letterhead.
2. Before emailing it, check the current Mitacs application portal or current program instructions for the designated upload/request workflow.
3. If the application explicitly instructs the referee to email the letter, use the exact recipient, subject format, and file naming it provides. The referee should send it from their institutional email if that is required or requested.
4. If the portal provides a referee-upload link, use that workflow instead of sending an unsolicited email.
5. Keep the final signed PDF and the application confirmation in this private `docs` folder; do not commit it to a public repository.

## Material to give the referee

- Current CV: [`../Sheikh_Abdullah_Bin_Zahid_Research_CV.pdf`](../Sheikh_Abdullah_Bin_Zahid_Research_CV.pdf)
- A short, factual achievement brief tailored to the referee's firsthand knowledge.
- The project/research areas being pursued and the application deadline once confirmed.

Never ask a referee to state claims they cannot personally support. The strongest letter is specific, comparative, and grounded in work they observed.
